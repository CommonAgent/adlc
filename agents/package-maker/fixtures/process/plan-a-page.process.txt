PROCESS: plan a page    id: example/plan-a-page v1
owner: owner@example.com   effective: 2026-11-01
trigger: a person hands over the address of a page to plan from
         carries: url - the page's address, required
         carries: angle - what the person wants to think about, optional
concurrency: runs may overlap - one run per page
goal: a plan of lenses for the page, its central claim named and each lens
      tied to the page's own words, approved by the owner

phases:
  read-the-page       - runs read-site: the page and the same-site pages it
                        links to, each page's words in order
                        owner: site-reader
                        after: trigger    automation: autonomous
  plan-the-lenses     - runs plan-lenses: the page's central claim, the
                        facts it states in its own words, and the lenses
                        that suit the angle
                        owner: ponder-planner
                        after: read-the-page    automation: autonomous
  approve-the-plan    - human: the owner reads the plan and says yes, asks
                        for changes, or turns it down
                        owner: owner@example.com
                        after: plan-the-lenses    automation: never

handoffs:
  read-the-page -> plan-the-lenses [site-read]: the page's words, in order
  plan-the-lenses -> approve-the-plan [plan]: the central claim and the
    lenses, each with the facts it rests on

deviations:
  approve-the-plan -> plan-the-lenses [changes]: the owner names what is
    wrong with the plan; the lenses are planned again

bindings:
  roster: site-reader and ponder-planner are agents, named in the roster
      the owner sends with the run; the owner signs the plan
  systems: none of the phases reaches a system of its own; each agent
      brings what its package declares
  data: the page's address and the angle

policy:
  a lens rests only on facts in the page's own words; nothing is
      published or sent outside the run

measures:
  cycle time: from the address to the approved plan
  quality gate: every approved plan names the page's central claim and
      each of its lenses cites the page's own words
